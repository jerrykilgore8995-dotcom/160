# Mandatory circuit and harmonic validation
Status: requirements only; no simulation results yet.
## Completeness
Transcribe every populated resistor, capacitor, diode, transistor, IC/module, potentiometer, trimmer, switch, transformer winding, rectifier, meter and LED from the selected original schematic revision. Include all +24 VDC circuitry, discrete transistor output stage, supply loading, rail impedance, ripple, decoupling, ground returns, and metering interactions. Verify actual rail and bias voltages against documentation. Expand original Blackmer VCA and RMS detector internals wherever documented. Record each reference designator, exact nodes, value, model source, tolerances and unresolved uncertainty. Do not claim completeness while any required part remains unresolved. No simulation before complete connectivity audit.
## Numerical integrity
- Use double precision or better for reference calculations. Record solver tolerances, integration method, timestep, convergence failures, sample grids and all initial conditions.
- Settle DC bias and detector state before measuring. Use integer-cycle coherent sine records; verify fundamental amplitude and phase, remove DC only for AC spectral calculation. Resample adaptive SPICE output on a uniform grid with verified interpolation convergence, or fit harmonic sinusoids on original timestamps with appropriate time weighting.
- Independently cross-check FFT results against least-squares harmonic extraction. Test ideal sine and known polynomial nonlinearities through the same pipeline to quantify numerical floor and recover calibrated amplitudes. Target floor below -130 dBc for ideal synthetic signals, or at least 20 dB below each claimed harmonic; report unresolved bins as upper bounds, not exact levels.
- Sweep maximum timestep and solver tolerances until significant complex harmonic coefficients and THD converge; compare trapezoidal and Gear methods as diagnostic, not as a means to suppress genuine distortion. At least three refinements; record actual differences. Never conceal spurs with a spectral floor clamp.
- Distinguish coherent harmonics, mains ripple, intermodulation, broadband noise, aliasing and solver artifacts. Evaluate deterministic distortion with stochastic noise disabled, then evaluate noise and THD+N with noise enabled. Record hum separately and account for overlapping bins.
- For K2–K20, ensure internal sample rate and time resolution cover 20*f0; at 20 kHz this is 400 kHz. A 44.1 kHz plugin output cannot contain the full harmonic series for high test fundamentals: report in-band harmonics separately from internal harmonics and folded products. Use anti-aliasing and independent oversampling convergence tests.
## Circuit attribution
Capture input, VCA current output, I/V stage, RMS detector log/control nodes, gain-control voltage and final output, using nonloading probes. Compare full circuit to diagnostic runs with detector control frozen at matched steady operating point and idealized rails at matched DC values; do not use these diagnostic changes in the reference circuit. Compare complex harmonic coefficients, not scalar subtraction of dB spectra. Preserve legitimate RMS ripple and its coupling into the VCA. Investigate any anomalous near-flat K2–K20 ladder through convergence, clipping, discontinuities, interpolation, model validity and spectral extraction. Do not force detector THD to zero or impose a manually chosen ladder.
## Required operating matrix
20, 50, 100, 500 Hz, 1, 5, 10, 20 kHz; multiple input levels, threshold positions, ratios and output settings; 0, 3, 5, 10, 20, 30 dB gain reduction where attainable; bridging and 600-ohm load. Include two-tone and transient tests, supply ripple and output clipping. Calibrate digital-to-analog level mapping and disclose it. Compare to original factory conditions, not arbitrary dBFS equivalences.
Report K2–K20 amplitudes in dBc relative to measured fundamental, phases, THD in percent and dB, THD+N with stated bandwidth, noise spectrum, raw waveforms and solver convergence tables. Compare factory published distortion under its stated limiting conditions; do not treat unspecified harmonic spectra as known. Hardware measurement is necessary to establish actual unit-specific harmonic agreement where published data is absent.
Release blocked until coverage, numerical validation, circuit attribution, real-time DSP comparison, and Windows VST3 tests pass. No static harmonic injection or HNM residual duplication to force a THD match.
